PHOSPHORWX

Aviation METAR & TAF Weather, Retro Style.

Check live aviation weather from 1,200+ airports worldwide through the warm glow of a vintage phosphor terminal. PhosphorWX pulls real-time METAR observations and TAF forecasts from NOAA's AviationWeather.gov and renders them in authentic green or amber monochrome, complete with scanline overlays, decoded weather data, and flight category indicators.

FEATURES

  • Live METAR observations for 1,200+ airports worldwide
  • TAF terminal aerodrome forecasts with decoded periods
  • Flight category indicators (VFR, MVFR, IFR, LIFR)
  • Raw and decoded weather data display
  • Continent-based airport filtering with search
  • Favourite airports list for quick access
  • Green phosphor and amber CRT colour themes
  • iOS home screen widgets
  • Runs natively on iPad, with the airport list beside the weather detail
  • No accounts, no tracking, no clutter

DATA SOURCE

All weather data is sourced from AviationWeather.gov, operated by NOAA's National Weather Service. This is US government public domain data with no copyright restrictions.
