PHOSPHORWX — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: February 2026

DATA COLLECTION

PhosphorWX does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, registration, or sign-in of any kind.

NETWORK REQUESTS

PhosphorWX connects to the AviationWeather.gov API (operated by NOAA's National Weather Service) to retrieve live METAR and TAF weather data. These requests are made directly from your device to the NOAA servers. No data passes through any intermediary server operated by us.

ANALYTICS AND TRACKING

PhosphorWX does not include any analytics frameworks, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind.

LOCAL STORAGE

PhosphorWX stores your preferences (such as favourite airports and colour theme) locally on your device using standard iOS application storage. This data never leaves your device.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The only external service PhosphorWX communicates with is AviationWeather.gov, operated by NOAA's National Weather Service. This is US government public domain data. Their use of any data is governed by their own policies.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

PhosphorWX does not collect data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.

CONTACT

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the PhosphorWX GitHub repository.
